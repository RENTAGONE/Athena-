## Key design constraints
The learner independently studies using external sources. ATHENA assigns objectives and evidence requirements; it does not attempt to replace all books, courses, instructors or primary sources. Mastery is not awarded from lesson completion or AI output. Evidence must include provenance, closed-book explanation, application, uncertainty and reflection. The learner approves any persistent update.
