## Research method and evidence interpretation

Each record has a skill-specific evidence-fit label, source mapping, practice refinement, assessment refinement and limitation. Sources are mapped by task family; they do not establish causal efficacy for every exact ATHENA micro-skill. Official safety guidance supports boundaries and safe decisions, not self-certification.

## Category synthesis

### Navigation & situational awareness

Teach map scale, orientation, cardinal directions, bearings and route checkpoints as separate observable subskills. The USGS materials directly support map/compass concepts; actual safe urban travel requires local route context, guardian-approved plans and supervised scenario practice. A compass exercise is not permission to navigate alone.

### Workshop & tool handling

Separate recognition, inspection, selection, workholding, measurement, hand-tool force and low-voltage circuit assembly. OSHA material is authoritative workplace safety guidance but must be adapted to a young learner and not interpreted as authorization for power tools. Keep electrical tasks battery-powered extra-low-voltage only with suitable adult supervision.

### Movement & sport technique

Treat movement skills as technique under controlled progression, not maximal performance. Youth physical activity guidance and age/stage-specific coaching support varied, enjoyable activity and safe development; they do not supply a universal sprint, landing or aerobic pass threshold. Score movement quality and symptoms separately.

### Photography & visual capture

Use controlled comparison sets and one-variable-at-a-time experiments. Adobe instructional materials support composition/focus/exposure practice, but aesthetic judgement needs a transparent rubric and cannot be treated as objective competence without context.

### Vocal & auditory performance

Separate pitch/timing perception, vocal production, speech pacing/projection and recording technique. Clinical voice guidance supports comfortable use and avoiding strain; it does not validate singing technique. Keep all practice comfortable, brief and interruptible.

### Household & food skills

Safety dominates convenience. Food-safety and WHO hand-hygiene guidance can support clean/separate/cook/chill and handwashing. Product-date rules differ by jurisdiction and product; ATHENA should teach label interpretation and local guardian guidance, not import US rules as Bangladesh law. Cleaning products must never be mixed.

### Personal operations & planning

Teach planning as a feedback loop: define outcome, next action, estimate, contingency, decision rationale, handoff, inventory and clean closure. Checklists should scaffold memory and coordination without replacing understanding. Time estimates should be calibrated against actual task data.

### Legacy / special-interest skills

Preserve user-selected skills as optional interests, but do not overstate transfer. Method of loci has promising recall results but low/very-low quality evidence overall in the recent meta-analysis; speed reading must preserve comprehension; lucid-dream practice must not disrupt sleep; locksport is limited to owned transparent practice locks; lighter handling is hazard literacy only.

### MS-08-001 — Orient a map to the environment
- **Evidence fit:** DIRECT TASK INSTRUCTION OR TECHNICAL GUIDANCE + INDIRECT MOTOR/PRACTICE MECHANISM; task-specific efficacy not established
- **Sources:** SRC-W2-LRN-01, SRC-W2-NAV-01, SRC-W2-NAV-02
- **Practice refinement:** Practise first with paper maps, mock routes and scenario cards in a familiar safe setting. Require the learner to stop, orient, identify stable landmarks, compare route options and select a guardian-approved safe response. Never send a learner alone to test navigation.

## Key design decisions

- Keep all 86 records DRAFT.
- Treat the catalogue/registry count mismatch as a data-integrity issue to preserve in provenance, not silently overwrite.
- Do not force all candidates into the same practice dose or threshold.
- For safety-sensitive navigation, workshop, sport, food, chemical and fire-related tasks, ATHENA should teach recognition, stop rules and escalation before any practical progression.
- Defer expert review and learner testing until the app can present and record the actual learning experience.
