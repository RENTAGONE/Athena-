## Completion standard (unchanged)
A discipline cannot be called complete merely because it has a macro-map, skill-family list, or a small number of teaching units. Completion requires defensible scope; prerequisite/progression coverage; substantive instruction; assessment and evidence criteria; practical/transfer opportunities; safety, ethics and accessibility review; verified source pathways; learner pilot evidence; an accepted residual-gap register; and stable schema/import tests.
