## Completion standard (must not be weakened)
A discipline cannot be called complete merely because it has a macro-map, skill-family list, or a small number of teaching units. Completion requires: (1) defensible scope and boundaries; (2) prerequisite and progression coverage; (3) substantive instructional units across the required breadth; (4) assessment coverage and valid evidence criteria; (5) practical/transfer opportunities where appropriate; (6) safety, ethics and accessibility review; (7) verified source/resource pathways; (8) learner pilot evidence; (9) unresolved-gap register with accepted residual risks; and (10) stable schema/import tests.

## Status vocabulary
- `AUTHORED_DRAFT`: content exists but has not passed internal checks.
- `INTERNALLY_CHECKED`: structural and editorial checks completed; not external validation.
- `SOURCE_CHECKED`: material claims and referenced resources independently checked.
- `EXPERT_REVIEWED`: qualified subject review documented.
- `LEARNER_VALIDATED`: tested with learners and revised from evidence.
- `APPROVED`: owner-approved release state with versioned provenance.

No stage may be silently promoted. Missing evidence remains an open gap.
