# ATHENA Micro-skill Library — Candidate Catalogue v1.0 Working

**Candidate count:** 155
**Status:** Draft candidates; not all have full protocols, prerequisites or verified assessment rubrics yet.

## What counts as a micro-skill
A micro-skill is a bounded, independently trainable capability with a clear performance target and a practical test. It is not automatically mastered in a weekend, and it is not necessarily permanent. Some can be acquired quickly; dexterity, communication, motor and safety skills often require longer practice and maintenance.

## Retention profiles
- **D1 — Durable routine:** may remain available for a long time after strong acquisition. Confirm with delayed unaided performance; annual refresh is only a possible maintenance interval after evidence supports it.
- **D2 — Moderate decay:** requires periodic retrieval and use in varied contexts.
- **D3 — Fluency / motor / social performance:** commonly needs more frequent short practice to preserve speed, accuracy and adaptability.
- **D4 — Safety-critical:** requires current certified training and practical refresh. A yearly hour of solo revision is not enough to claim readiness.

A review interval is earned by performance evidence, not assigned from category alone. First learn, then test after a meaningful delay, then widen the interval only when performance remains reliable.

## Completion rules
- Do not mark a candidate mastered from reading or a single immediate attempt.
- Require independent performance and at least one delayed check; use multiple contexts where transfer matters.
- Preserve the original evidence if performance later declines. Set current status to `needs_revision` and record relearning evidence.
- Safety-critical capabilities are not self-certified by ATHENA. Use recognised, current training and qualified assessment.
- Expand each candidate into a full record before approval: prerequisites, equipment, step-by-step practice protocol, error cues, difficulty variants, assessment rubric, safety constraints, retention schedule and links to macro skills.
