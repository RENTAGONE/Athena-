# Resource Pathways — Message 18 Addendum

**Scope:** New candidate source links added to the 216-module crosswalk. These are not a declaration that the resources have been fully read, expert-reviewed or approved.

## New verified publisher/host sources

- **Engineering & Computer Science:** Harvard CS50x for problem decomposition, algorithms, data structures, programming, SQL and web foundations; Pro Git and MDN remain the more direct sources for version control and web documentation.
- **Business:** OpenStax business textbook catalogue, including Business Ethics, Entrepreneurship and Introduction to Business. It is a general business source; Bangladesh law and market practice require local sources.
- **Electronics:** Existing All About Circuits textbook mapped to resistors/LEDs, diodes, transistor switching, capacitors, breadboard prototyping and digital logic. Microcontroller depth remains partial.
- **Body Science:** CDC adolescent sleep and youth activity guidance; MedlinePlus health-information evaluation. CDC recommendations are US public-health guidance, not personal medical prescriptions.
- **Psychology:** Existing OpenStax Psychology 2e mapped to social psychology and as related background for systems/causal reasoning.
- **Workflow:** Google Workspace Learning Center, Microsoft File Explorer support, TypingClub, Google Apps Script samples and Google Drive file recovery.
- **Art:** Draw Practice Lab supports line, shape, proportion, perspective, value, colour and basic anatomy exercises. Its scoring is not independently validated and mouse/Apple Pencil may be required.
- **Cybersecurity:** Cisco Networking Academy for networking and cybersecurity foundations; Cisco Linux Essentials for Linux/command-line foundations; Google Drive restore is only a narrow file-recovery example, not a full backup plan.
- **Language:** British Council writing practice and Bangladesh Accessible Dictionary for Bengali-English lookup. A dictionary does not replace specialist terminology sources.
- **Practical Independence:** BDRCS first-aid training course, Bangladesh DDM AWARE emergency/disaster information, Bangladesh Railway schedules, CAB consumer-rights context, ACCC garment-care labels and BFSA food-safety search result.
- **Body Science ethics:** OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology for biological context plus UNESCO human-genome declaration for dignity, non-discrimination, consent and privacy principles.

## Scope limitations

- **Five modules remain unmapped** because the currently checked sources do not adequately cover human variation/ethics and several specific curriculum outcomes. They are intentionally not force-mapped.
- The BFSA official page fetch timed out; the record remains `unreviewed` and its module link is only a provisional related candidate.
- US, UK and Australian sources are not Bangladesh legal or regulatory authority. The ACCC clothing-label page is a symbol reference, not local law.
- BDRCS training is provider-led. Confirm current fees, eligibility and dates with BDRCS and a guardian; reading a page does not confer first-aid competence.
- Resource exposure is not evidence of learning or mastery.
