## DEEP-MATH-001 — Number systems, magnitude and estimation

**Objective:** Represent integers, fractions, decimals, percentages and rational values; compare magnitude and estimate before calculating.

**Core explanation:** Number systems, magnitude and estimation is developed through explicit models, worked reasoning, deliberate practice and independent transfer. The learner should understand both the procedure and the conditions under which it is valid.

**Practice:** Place mixed values on a number line; estimate shopping totals, ratios and measurement conversions.

**Assessment:** Without a calculator, estimate then calculate a multi-step quantity and explain the size check.

**Success criteria:**
- Performs independently in a new but appropriately scoped task.
- Explains the reasoning and checks the result.
- Identifies assumptions, limits and common failure modes.

**Misconceptions:** Confusing operation rules with magnitude; treating decimal digits as whole numbers.

**Retrieval:** Without notes, explain the central model of Number systems, magnitude and estimation and reconstruct its key steps.

**Delayed check:** Reassess after a meaningful delay selected from observed performance; do not assume a universal interval.

**Transfer:** Apply Number systems, magnitude and estimation to a different context and explain what transfers and what does not.
