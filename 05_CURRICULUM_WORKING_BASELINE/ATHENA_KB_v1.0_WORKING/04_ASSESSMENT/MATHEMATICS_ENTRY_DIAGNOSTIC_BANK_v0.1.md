# ATHENA Mathematics — Entry Diagnostic Item Bank v0.1

**Items:** 34 (two per scope strand). **Status:** authored pilot bank; not psychometrically calibrated.
**Use:** low-stakes placement and gap discovery. Do not interpret raw score as a global mathematics ability label. Permit oral or accessible equivalent responses where appropriate.

## Items and answer guidance

### MAT-01 Numeracy
1. Estimate 19.8×51 without exact multiplication.
**Answer guidance:** Approximately 1,000; acceptable range 950–1,050 with a stated rounding strategy.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests magnitude rather than exact arithmetic.

### MAT-01 Numeracy
2. Which is greater, 5/8 or 0.61? Justify.
**Answer guidance:** 5/8=0.625, so 5/8 is greater by 0.015.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests representation conversion and comparison.

### MAT-02 Algebra
3. Simplify 3(2x−5)−2(x+4).
**Answer guidance:** 6x−15−2x−8=4x−23.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests distribution and like terms.

### MAT-02 Algebra
4. Solve 5−2x=13 and check.
**Answer guidance:** −2x=8, x=−4; substitution gives 5+8=13.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests equality-preserving operations and checking.

### MAT-03 Functions
5. Is {(1,2),(2,2),(1,3)} a function? Why?
**Answer guidance:** No. Input 1 has two outputs, 2 and 3.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests function definition.

### MAT-03 Functions
6. State the real domain of f(x)=√(x+4)/(x−1).
**Answer guidance:** Require x+4≥0 and x≠1; domain [−4,∞) excluding 1.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests combined domain restrictions.

### MAT-04 Geometry
7. A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm and length 9 cm. Find width and area.
**Answer guidance:** 2(9+w)=30, w=6 cm; area=54 cm².
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests formula use and units.

### MAT-04 Geometry
8. Explain why two triangles with equal corresponding angles are similar, not necessarily congruent.
**Answer guidance:** Equal angles fix shape; scale may differ, so corresponding sides are proportional but not necessarily equal.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests conceptual distinction.

### MAT-05 Trigonometry
9. A right triangle has opposite 8 and hypotenuse 10. Find sinθ and cosθ.
**Answer guidance:** sinθ=4/5; adjacent=6 by Pythagoras, so cosθ=3/5.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests ratios and Pythagoras.

### MAT-05 Trigonometry
10. Convert 3π/4 radians to degrees.
**Answer guidance:** 3π/4×180/π=135°.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests angle units.

### MAT-06 Sequences
11. Find the 10th term of 4, 7, 10, 13, …
**Answer guidance:** Arithmetic difference 3; a₁₀=4+9×3=31.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests arithmetic sequence indexing.

### MAT-06 Sequences
12. Find the sum of the first 5 terms of 2, 6, 18, …
**Answer guidance:** Geometric ratio 3; S₅=2(3⁵−1)/(3−1)=242.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests geometric series formula.

### MAT-07 Probability
13. A fair six-sided die is rolled. What is P(even)?
**Answer guidance:** 3/6=1/2.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests sample space.

### MAT-07 Probability
14. If P(A)=0.4, P(B)=0.5 and events are independent, find P(A∩B).
**Answer guidance:** 0.4×0.5=0.2. Independence is essential.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests conditional assumptions.

### MAT-08 Statistics
15. For data 2, 3, 3, 4, 100, compare mean and median.
**Answer guidance:** Mean=22.4; median=3. The extreme value pulls the mean upward.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests robust summaries and outliers.

### MAT-08 Statistics
16. A poll samples only people entering a gym. What bias may affect a claim about all adults?
**Answer guidance:** Selection/coverage bias: gym entrants are not representative of all adults.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests sampling validity.

### MAT-09 Calculus
17. Differentiate f(x)=3x²−4x+7.
**Answer guidance:** f′(x)=6x−4.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests power rule.

### MAT-09 Calculus
18. Interpret derivative as a rate in a position-time model.
**Answer guidance:** Instantaneous velocity is the derivative of position with respect to time; units are distance/time.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests conceptual meaning and units.

### MAT-10 Linear algebra
19. Solve x+y=5, x−y=1.
**Answer guidance:** Add: 2x=6, x=3, y=2.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests system representation.

### MAT-10 Linear algebra
20. What does multiplying a vector by 2 do geometrically?
**Answer guidance:** Doubles its magnitude while preserving direction (unless zero vector, whose direction is undefined).
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests scalar multiplication.

### MAT-11 Discrete mathematics
21. Negate “Every student submitted the assignment.”
**Answer guidance:** “At least one student did not submit the assignment.”
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests quantifier negation.

### MAT-11 Discrete mathematics
22. A graph has 5 vertices and is a tree. How many edges?
**Answer guidance:** 4 edges; every finite tree with n vertices has n−1 edges.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests graph theory invariant.

### MAT-12 Number theory
23. Find gcd(84,30).
**Answer guidance:** 6. For example, 84=2²×3×7 and 30=2×3×5.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests factorisation.

### MAT-12 Number theory
24. What is 17 mod 5?
**Answer guidance:** 2, since 17=3×5+2.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests congruence remainder.

### MAT-13 Numerical methods
25. A measured length is 12.4 cm to nearest 0.1 cm. Give a reasonable rounding interval.
**Answer guidance:** At conventional nearest-tenth rounding, actual value is approximately [12.35,12.45) cm, subject to instrument/calibration uncertainty.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests precision interpretation.

### MAT-13 Numerical methods
26. Why can repeated numerical iteration fail to converge?
**Answer guidance:** The iteration may be unstable, the chosen method/initial value unsuitable, or the problem may not satisfy convergence conditions.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests limits of computation.

### MAT-14 Optimisation
27. Maximise x+y subject to x≥0, y≥0, x+2y≤8, 2x+y≤10. Identify candidate corner points.
**Answer guidance:** Feasible corners: (0,0),(5,0),(4,2),(0,4). Objective values 0,5,6,4; maximum 6 at (4,2).
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests feasible-region optimisation.

### MAT-14 Optimisation
28. Why is an unconstrained maximum not necessarily feasible in a real problem?
**Answer guidance:** It may violate resource, non-negativity, integer, safety or policy constraints.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests constraints.

### MAT-15 Modelling
29. A car travels 150 km in 2.5 h at constant speed. Find average speed.
**Answer guidance:** 60 km/h. Constant speed is an assumption if used for every instant.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests units and model assumptions.

### MAT-15 Modelling
30. Give one reason a linear forecast may fail over a long horizon.
**Answer guidance:** Rate may change, saturation/constraints may occur, or the process may be nonlinear; answer must connect to context.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests model limitations.

### MAT-16 Proof
31. Give a counterexample to “all prime numbers are odd.”
**Answer guidance:** 2 is prime and even.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests counterexample reasoning.

### MAT-16 Proof
32. Is the converse of “If n is divisible by 4, then n is even” true?
**Answer guidance:** No. n=2 is even but not divisible by 4.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests converse fallacy.

### MAT-17 Practice
33. A calculator returns 0.0000003 for a quantity expected to be around 300. What should you do before accepting it?
**Answer guidance:** Check units, input, parentheses, scale, scientific notation and estimate independently; do not assume calculator output is correct.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests verification habits.

### MAT-17 Practice
34. When should a computer algebra system not replace a written explanation?
**Answer guidance:** When the objective is to demonstrate reasoning, justify a transformation, explain assumptions or communicate a proof; tool output can support but not substitute for that evidence.
**Diagnostic purpose:** Tests responsible tool use.

## Administration and interpretation

- Use as a sampling instrument, not a high-stakes exam. A learner may skip unfamiliar advanced items; unfamiliarity is not a moral or general-ability judgement.
- Record each item by strand and evidence dimension: concept, procedure, strategy, reasoning, modelling, communication, calibration.
- A correct answer with no requested reasoning may be marked partially evidenced, not fully demonstrated.
- Follow incorrect responses with a short interview: ask the learner to explain the first step and identify what they are unsure about.
- Do not set a universal cut score. Interpret patterns by prerequisite clusters and verify with additional parallel items.
- Before operational use, obtain educator review, check ambiguity and accessibility, pilot with learners, analyse item difficulty and revise.
