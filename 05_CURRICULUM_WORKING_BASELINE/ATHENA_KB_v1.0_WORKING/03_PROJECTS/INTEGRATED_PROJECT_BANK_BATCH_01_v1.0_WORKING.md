# ATHENA Integrated Project Bank — Batch 01

**Status:** Draft project specifications. Select projects according to learner readiness, supervision and available resources.

## P-01 — Build and operate a tiny digital product

**Disciplines:** ENG;BIZ;FIN;COMM;RES;WORK

**Deliverables:** A working prototype, customer/problem brief, cost ledger, test log and short demo.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: user task completion, reproducible build, clear price/cost assumptions, source traceability and a documented revision.

## P-02 — Design a low-voltage sensor experiment

**Disciplines:** ELEC;MATH;RES;SCI;WORK

**Deliverables:** A safe low-voltage circuit/simulation, measurement table, graph and uncertainty note.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: circuit diagram matches build, units correct, repeat readings, uncertainty discussed and safety boundary observed.

## P-03 — Create a personal learning module

**Disciplines:** LEARN;MATH;LANG;RES;PSY;COMM

**Deliverables:** A lesson, retrieval prompts, worked example, delayed test and revision note.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: novice can complete target task; assessment measures objective; delayed recall is recorded.

## P-04 — Produce a visual identity and accessible landing page

**Disciplines:** AEST;ENG;BIZ;COMM;RES

**Deliverables:** Brand rationale, responsive page, accessibility checks and user feedback.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: hierarchy, readable mobile layout, accessible state cues and clear value proposition.

## P-05 — Plan and run a small ethical sales experiment

**Disciplines:** BIZ;FIN;MATH;RES;COMM

**Deliverables:** Offer hypothesis, customer interview notes, price test, transaction ledger and results memo.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: consent-based research, transparent claims, complete costs, no misleading scarcity and honest interpretation.

## P-06 — Football performance analysis notebook

**Disciplines:** ATH;MATH;RES;PSY;WORK

**Deliverables:** A drill plan, observation rubric, performance log and reflection.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: metrics defined before practice, sample limits acknowledged, workload/recovery considered and decisions linked to observations.

## P-07 — Phone photography field study

**Disciplines:** AEST;ART;RES;WORK;COMM

**Deliverables:** A 10-image series with composition notes, light comparisons and edited exports.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: intentional framing, consistent metadata, preserved originals, source/permission awareness and critique.

## P-08 — Household independence challenge

**Disciplines:** INDEP;MATH;COMM;WORK;BODY

**Deliverables:** A supervised meal plan, shopping calculation, preparation checklist and clean-up record.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: budget arithmetic, hygiene, allergen awareness, safe supervision and repeatability.

## P-09 — Research a public claim

**Disciplines:** RES;MATH;PSY;LANG;COMM

**Deliverables:** A question, search log, source matrix, claim-evidence map and qualified conclusion.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: primary sources where appropriate, citations, alternative explanations and limits.

## P-10 — Design a safe workshop organiser

**Disciplines:** ENG;AEST;MATH;INDEP;WORK

**Deliverables:** A measured design, parts list, prototype and user test.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: dimensions and tolerances, material suitability, tool safety, iteration and reproducible build notes.

## P-11 — Second-language mini immersion

**Disciplines:** LANG;COMM;PSY;WORK;RES

**Deliverables:** A two-week input/output plan, vocabulary set, recorded interaction and error analysis.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: balanced skills, retrieval after delay, comprehensible task completion and no streak-based mastery.

## P-12 — Community information explainer

**Disciplines:** RES;COMM;AEST;LANG;LEAD

**Deliverables:** A plain-language explainer, visual, source list and feedback revisions.

**Evidence and review:** Evidence: factual traceability, accessible design, audience comprehension and transparent uncertainty.
