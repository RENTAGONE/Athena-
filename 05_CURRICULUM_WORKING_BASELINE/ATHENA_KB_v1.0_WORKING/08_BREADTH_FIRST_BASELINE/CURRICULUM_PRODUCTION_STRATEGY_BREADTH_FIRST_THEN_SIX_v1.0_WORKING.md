# ATHENA Curriculum Production Strategy — Breadth First, Then Six at a Time

**Status:** User-approved production direction, 2026-11-17.

## Why this sequence

The curriculum should not leave twelve disciplines as empty shells while six become extremely deep. First establish a minimum coherent baseline across all 18 disciplines. Then deepen six disciplines at a time until each selected wave has real, connected pathways rather than isolated modules.

## Phase I — Universal baseline across all 18

The first baseline requires, for every discipline:
- domain and skill-family coverage;
- essential concepts and prerequisite relationships;
- beginner-to-functional learning sequence;
- observable learning objectives;
- guided and independent practice;
- retrieval, explanation, application and transfer assessments;
- misconception checks and evidence criteria;
- links to shared foundations, micro-skills and integrated projects;
- safety and age-appropriateness review where relevant.

Batch I provides 12 baseline modules per discipline (216 total). These are a first pass and must be reviewed, expanded and mapped to the 145-domain/733-family macro scaffold. Do not mistake 12 modules per discipline for complete coverage of every domain.

## Phase II — Deep authoring in three six-discipline waves

### Wave 1 — Quantitative, technical and evidence foundations
1. Mathematics & Scientific Reasoning (MATH)
2. Engineering & Computer Science (ENG)
3. Electronics & Hardware (ELEC)
4. Research & Information Intelligence (RES)
5. Language & Linguistic Intelligence (LANG)
6. Communication & Rhetoric (COMM)

**Reason:** These provide reusable foundations for later work: quantitative reasoning, computing, measurement, evidence, comprehension and explanation.

### Wave 2 — Economic, operational and social systems
1. Business & Entrepreneurship (BIZ)
2. Finance & Economics (FIN)
3. Workflow & Productivity (WORK)
4. Cybersecurity & Digital Infrastructure (CYBER)
5. Psychology & Strategy (PSY)
6. Leadership & Management (LEAD)

**Reason:** These disciplines build on the first wave's analytical, technical, research and communication foundations.

### Wave 3 — Human performance, creative practice and independence
1. Body Science & Anatomy (BODY)
2. Athletics & Physical Performance (ATH)
3. Vocal & Performance Arts (PERF)
4. Aesthetics & Visual Design (AEST)
5. Artistic Development (ART)
6. Practical Independence (INDEP)

**Reason:** These combine scientific literacy, deliberate practice, communication, design and practical application.

## Deep-completion definition for each six-discipline wave

A discipline is not considered deeply authored merely because it has many modules. The wave must include:
1. reviewed domain → skill-family → skill → subskill decomposition;
2. concept graph and justified prerequisite graph;
3. complete foundational, functional, intermediate and advanced pathways;
4. learning units with explanation, worked examples, guided practice, independent practice and authentic application;
5. objective-aligned assessment banks and rubrics;
6. evidence rules for independent demonstration, delayed retention and transfer;
7. capability-specific maintenance and relearning plans;
8. verified resource records with provenance and access/verification dates;
9. at least one integrated project per major domain cluster;
10. cross-disciplinary transfer links with mechanism and limitations;
11. safety, ethics, accessibility and age-appropriate review;
12. schema, ID, reference, graph and import validation.

## Governance

- Do not make all suggested sequence links hard prerequisites.
- Preserve diagnostic placement and allow learners to test out of known material.
- Do not fabricate resource URLs or imply expert review that did not occur.
- Keep micro-skills distinct from macro disciplines while linking them to relevant skills and projects.
- Ambidexterity remains a longitudinal capability, not a short completion badge.
- Legacy micro-skills (Rubik's Cube, origami, safe locksport practice, Stadium Whistle, Doomsday Algorithm, Memory Palace, Morse Code, French Drop, shuffles, Signature Dish, knots, CPR/AED and choking-response training) remain preserved with their existing safety boundaries.
- The Master Vault is the canonical project memory; the Knowledge Architecture Pack is the canonical ontology/schema package; the v1.0 working curriculum is a separate authored-content layer.

## Release principle

The user requested a substantial first deployment baseline, approximately 70–99% complete in practical coverage, with later expansion in v2.0. Report completeness by coverage and validation dimensions, never by an invented single percentage. A v1.0 release must be importable and usable, with known limitations documented.
