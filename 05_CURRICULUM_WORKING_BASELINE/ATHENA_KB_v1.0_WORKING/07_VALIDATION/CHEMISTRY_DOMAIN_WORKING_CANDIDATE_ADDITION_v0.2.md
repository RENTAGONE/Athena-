# Chemistry working-domain candidate addition v0.2

**Status:** Added to the expanded working scaffold as `DOM-MATH-09`; canonical `ATH-DOM-MATH-CHEM` remains unchanged and the mapping is still unapproved.

The working domain contains eight family candidates: Matter, Measurement & Classification; Atomic Structure & Periodic Patterns; Chemical Bonding & Molecular Structure; Chemical Formulae & Equations; Mole Concept & Stoichiometry; Solutions, Acids & Concentration; Chemical Energy, Rates & Equilibrium; Experimental Chemistry & Data.

This addition raises the working scaffold from 145 domains / 733 family entries to 146 domains / 741 family entries. The domain crosswalk now has candidate coverage for all 113 canonical domains. This is a candidate inventory improvement, not a canonical ontology approval.

Safety boundary from the original proposal remains binding: initial learning is conceptual/simulation-led; no home synthesis, heating, chemical mixing, gas generation, corrosive handling or unsupervised wet-lab work. Practical laboratory activity requires qualified supervision, PPE, risk assessment, ventilation and approved materials. Never mix cleaning products.
