## Definition of a genuinely complete discipline

A discipline is not complete because it has a domain list, 12 baseline IDs, or several teaching packs. It must pass all of these gates:

1. **Scope:** reviewed domain and family tree; explicit boundaries, omissions and interfaces with other disciplines.
2. **Progression:** entry, foundational, functional, intermediate, advanced and specialist pathways with prerequisites and placement rules.
3. **Instruction:** every active pathway has units with objectives, prerequisite checks, accurate explanations, worked examples, guided practice, independent practice, feedback, misconceptions and application.
4. **Assessment:** diagnostic, formative, cumulative, practical and transfer assessments with answer/rubric guidance and evidence requirements.
5. **Resources:** real sources verified for existence, relevance, level, access and licensing/usage constraints; no fabricated links.
6. **Retention:** item-specific recall and maintenance rules; not one universal review interval.
7. **Projects:** discipline-specific outputs and cross-discipline projects with observable evidence.
8. **Graph integrity:** stable IDs, prerequisite edges, concept links, cross-domain transfer links and no unsupported hard gates.
9. **Safety and ethics:** risk boundaries, supervision and qualification requirements where relevant.
10. **Review:** internal checks, independent subject review, learner pilot, corrections and recorded approval state.

External expert review and learner validation cannot be claimed by authoring alone. Until these gates pass, status remains IN PROGRESS.

## Status vocabulary

- `scope_mapped` — scope exists; instructional content not implied.
- `outline_authored` — a module outline exists.
- `instructional_draft` — full learning unit authored.
- `internally_checked` — specified internal checks passed.
- `source_checked` — cited resources/claims verified.
- `expert_reviewed` — independent qualified review recorded.
- `learner_validated` — pilot evidence and revisions recorded.
- `approved` — release owner accepts the reviewed content.

Never convert one status into another by counting files or modules.
