## Remaining baseline remediation

1. Author 12 substantive teaching units for each of the other 17 disciplines (204 modules).
2. Replace generic objectives, criteria, assessment modes, retention and safety metadata with capability-specific content.
3. Build task-specific answer keys, acceptable variants and analytic scoring anchors.
4. Verify sources, licensing and access details; distinguish primary references from optional practice platforms.
5. Run discipline review and learner pilots; do not promote draft status from structural checks alone.
6. Integrate all units into the canonical knowledge graph and validate prerequisite/transfer semantics.

## Authoring completion standard

Each module requires bounded scope, entry assumptions, learner-facing explanation, at least two reasoned worked examples, guided practice with answers, independent exercises with answer guidance, misconceptions with corrective feedback, task-specific assessment criteria, a changed-context transfer task, capability-specific retention design, and trustworthy sources. Risk-sensitive material additionally requires appropriate expert review.

## Limits

The audit verifies field-level structure and the presence of teaching packs. It does not certify mathematical correctness beyond the internal checks recorded for Batch 03, and it does not establish expert approval, learner comprehension, or application import compatibility.
