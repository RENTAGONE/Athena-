# Deepening Batch 01 — Content Quality Review

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** Structural authoring gate passed; instructional review remains open.

## Scope
18 units across Mathematics & Scientific Reasoning, Engineering & Computer Science, Electronics & Hardware, Research & Information Intelligence, Language & Linguistic Intelligence, and Communication & Rhetoric.

## Structural checks
- 18 unique stable unit IDs retained.
- Every unit has at least two worked examples with reasoning and result.
- Every unit has at least three guided practice prompts and five independent exercises.
- Every unit has a diagnostic placement item and common failure analysis.
- Every unit has a four-level rubric: emerging, developing, independent, transfer-ready.
- Every unit has prerequisite/follow-on candidates, a retention profile and a transfer task.

## Important limitations
This is not a claim of subject-expert validation. It has not been piloted with the learner, checked by a qualified electronics safety reviewer, independently fact-checked, linked to verified resources, reconciled into canonical prerequisite edges, or tested in the ATHENA importer. Electrical content is restricted to low-voltage educational contexts and still requires competent supervision.
