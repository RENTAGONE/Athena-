# Motion & Time-Based Design — Domain Extension Proposal v0.1

**Status:** Proposed AEST working-domain extension; no canonical ID minted and no existing domain changed.

## Why this may warrant a distinct domain

The working map contains a dedicated `Motion and Time-Based Design` domain but the current canonical domain map has no clear single target. Motion work combines temporal sequencing, movement, rhythm, transitions, keyframes, timing, continuity, compositing and delivery constraints. It overlaps with graphic design, interface design, illustration and performance, but cannot be reduced to static composition alone.

## Proposed scope

1. Time, duration, frame rate and temporal perception.
2. Keyframes, interpolation and easing.
3. Motion principles: anticipation, follow-through, staging, arcs and timing.
4. Typography and graphic motion.
5. Storyboarding, animatics and sequence planning.
6. Editing, transitions, continuity and pacing.
7. 2D/3D motion pipelines and compositing fundamentals.
8. Export, codecs, aspect ratios, accessibility and platform delivery.

## Boundaries

- Static visual hierarchy and identity systems remain in Graphic/Brand Design.
- Interactive state transitions and feedback remain in Interface/Interaction Design when the learning target is user interaction.
- Character animation and visual storytelling may cross-link with Artistic Development.
- Vocal/music performance timing remains in Performance Arts where sound/performance is the primary object.

## Proposed relationships

Connect to AEST Graphic & Brand Design, AEST Interface & Interaction Design, ART Illustration & Concept Art, PERF Performance Craft, ENG Web/Application Engineering and COMM Storytelling. These are proposed cross-domain links, not canonical edges.

## Decision required

Decide whether this is a distinct canonical AEST domain or a family cluster under an existing design domain. Review learning outcomes and production workflows before assigning an ID.
