## What this completion means

All 12 entry-baseline topics now have authored teaching-pack drafts. The final four include explicit entry knowledge, outcomes, concept explanations, worked examples, guided practice and answers, independent exercises and answer guidance, misconceptions and corrective moves, analytic assessment, transfer, retention/retrieval design and resource references. The first eight already have separate teaching packs; their metadata and assessment integration still require harmonisation against the current standard.

## What it does not mean

- It does not complete the entire Mathematics discipline or advanced mathematics pathway.
- It does not establish expert review or learner validation.
- It does not prove every resource link will remain available.
- It does not mean the 216-module baseline is complete: the other 204 modules remain outline-level.
- It does not mean app import or presentation has been tested.
