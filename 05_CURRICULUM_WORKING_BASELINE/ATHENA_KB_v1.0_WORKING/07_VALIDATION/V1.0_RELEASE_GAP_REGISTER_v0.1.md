## Current verified facts

- 18 macro disciplines are represented in the working curriculum.
- 216 baseline modules exist, 12 per discipline.
- 145 working domains and 733 skill-family entries exist in the expanded scaffold; they are not canonical.
- 155 micro-skill candidates exist with unique stable IDs.
- 155/155 candidate IDs appear in protocol documents; this is not equivalent to full protocol completion.
- 45 fuller stepwise draft protocols and 110 compact outlines are currently classified.
- 18 deepening units exist across Wave 1's six disciplines.
- 24 integrated project specifications exist.
- All content remains draft unless a record explicitly states otherwise; no expert approval, learner pilot or app import test is claimed.

## Release gate policy

A gap may be closed only with a recorded artefact, reproducible validation evidence and an explicit status change. A lexical match, generated count, draft heading or successful JSON parse is not sufficient evidence of semantic correctness or instructional completeness. Critical safety and importer gaps cannot be waived by content volume.
