The earlier audit treated 45 Batch 04 entries as fuller stepwise drafts. That was too generous: the document repeats generic setup, acquisition, practice-dose, failure-mode, retention and safety language across all 45 entries. The skill-specific assessment line is useful but does not make the full protocol skill-specific. Batch 03 contains 110 compact outlines. Batch 01 duplicates 10 entries already present in Batch 04. Twenty protocols now have substantive skill-specific drafts across cognitive and legacy capabilities. The remaining 135 registry candidates require substantive authoring/rework; none is approved or expert-reviewed.

## Required protocol acceptance standard

A protocol must be tailored to its actual skill. Generic verbs such as “watch a demonstration”, “practise the smallest component” or “vary one condition” are not sufficient unless translated into the exact actions, constraints, feedback and failure checks for that skill. Safety-critical items must specify qualified instruction and supervised competency assessment, not text-only completion.
