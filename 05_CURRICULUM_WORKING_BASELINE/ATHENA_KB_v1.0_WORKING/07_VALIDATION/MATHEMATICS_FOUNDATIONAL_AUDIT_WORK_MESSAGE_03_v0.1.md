**Scope:** BASE-MATH-001 through BASE-MATH-012 across Foundational Teaching Pack Batches 01–03.

## 1. Audit method

Each module was checked against the following minimum instructional contract:

1. Clear subject scope and learner-facing explanation.
2. Explicit entry knowledge and learning outcomes.
3. Worked examples that show reasoning rather than only answers.
4. Guided practice with answer guidance.
5. Independent practice with answer guidance.
6. Common misconceptions paired with corrective feedback.
7. Assessment criteria that evaluate reasoning and application.
8. Transfer task in a changed context.
9. Retrieval/retention or a clear exit decision.
10. Mathematical accuracy, units, domain restrictions and relevant edge cases.

A module can pass this structural audit while still needing an expert review for mathematical pedagogy, age appropriateness, local curriculum alignment and learner comprehension.

## 3. Corrections and extensions made

### BASE-MATH-003 — fraction division

The earlier explanation stated the reciprocal rule but did not provide a worked example or a direct independent check. Added `3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/2`, explained it as “how many halves fit into three quarters,” and verified by multiplying the quotient by the divisor. Added `2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 5/6` with a reverse-operation check.

### BASE-MATH-006 — signed multiplication

The earlier pack stated the sign pattern without showing why the negative-times-negative case is positive. Added a distributive-property argument, noted that division by zero is undefined, and added practice for zero and signed products/quotients.

### BASE-MATH-008 — solution-set edge cases

The earlier pack mainly modelled single-solution linear equations. Added one-solution, contradiction/no-solution and identity/infinitely-many-solution examples, plus an inequality solution-set interpretation. This prevents the learner from assuming every equation has one answer.

### Metadata harmonisation

Modules 001–008 now have explicit suggested workload, entry knowledge and measurable learning outcomes, matching the instructional metadata already used in Batch 03. Stable IDs and original module order are unchanged.

- BASE-MATH-004: a 12.5% discount on ৳1,600 is ৳200; final price ৳1,400; reverse 10% reduction from 450 gives 500.

- BASE-MATH-006: `−12 + 7 = −5`; `−8 × −3 = 24`; `36 ÷ −9 = −4`.
- BASE-MATH-007: `7m − 3 + 2m + 8 − 4m = 5m + 5`; `5(2p−3)` at p=2 equals 5.
- BASE-MATH-008: `−2x > 8` gives `x < −4`; identity and contradiction examples were checked.
- BASE-MATH-009–012: representative exponent, geometry, slope/function and proof examples were checked against the written answer guidance during the existing batch-03 internal QA; independent review remains outstanding.

These are spot checks, not exhaustive proof that every sentence and possible learner interpretation is error-free.

## 5. Remaining limitations and next actions

1. No independent mathematics educator has reviewed these packs.
2. No learner pilot has tested pacing, clarity, cognitive load or prerequisite assumptions.
3. Several concepts are introduced but not yet developed into full pathways: formal probability, combinatorics, statistics/inference, calculus beyond foundations, linear algebra, discrete mathematics, number theory, numerical methods and optimisation.
4. The packs need cumulative mixed practice so earlier skills are tested after later topics are introduced.
5. Answer guidance is not yet a fully separate worked-solution bank for every item.
6. Resource alignment, current link availability, edition details and licensing need a dedicated verification pass.
7. Bangladesh NCTB alignment and optional international pathway crosswalks need explicit mapping; this baseline is not a substitute for the learner's school syllabus.
