## Limitations and open review
- The units are not a complete probability/statistics sequence.
- Formal inference is introductory; sampling distributions, t procedures, chi-square, regression, design-based inference, Bayesian methods and statistical computing remain open.
- No psychometric calibration, expert review, learner pilot, accessibility audit or resource alignment certification has occurred.
- Confidence-interval interpretation follows the frequentist repeated-sampling framework; a Bayesian credible interval is a different object.
- The 1.5×IQR rule is a flag for investigation, not a rule to delete data.
- The illustrative Bayes screening example is mathematical, not medical guidance.

## Decision
Retain all four units as **substantive instructional drafts / internally calculation-checked**. Do not mark Mathematics complete, expert reviewed or learner validated.
