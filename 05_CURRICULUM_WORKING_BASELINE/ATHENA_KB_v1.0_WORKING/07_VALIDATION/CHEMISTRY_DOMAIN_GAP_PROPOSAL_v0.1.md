# Chemistry Foundations & Quantitative Modelling — Domain Gap Proposal v0.1

**Status:** Proposed working-domain addition; no stable ATHENA ID minted and no canonical record changed.

## Why this is a real gap

The canonical domain map includes `ATH-DOM-MATH-CHEM — Chemistry Foundations & Quantitative Modelling`, but the 145-domain working scaffold has no dedicated chemistry domain. Assigning chemistry to generic advanced mathematics would obscure its empirical, particulate, symbolic and laboratory dimensions. This proposal defines the missing scope before any record is added.

## Purpose

Build the mathematical and scientific literacy needed to reason about matter, chemical change, energy and quantitative composition. The domain should connect mathematics and scientific reasoning to chemistry without absorbing all of chemistry as a branch of mathematics.

## Proposed scope

1. **Matter and measurement:** substances/mixtures, states, physical properties, measurement, significant figures and uncertainty.
2. **Atomic structure:** protons, neutrons, electrons, isotopes, ions and evidence-based atomic models.
3. **Periodic patterns:** groups, periods, valence structure and cautious use of periodic trends.
4. **Chemical bonding and structure:** ionic, covalent and metallic models; molecular shape and intermolecular interactions at an appropriate level.
5. **Chemical language:** symbols, formulae, equations, states and naming conventions.
6. **Reactions and conservation:** reactants/products, balancing equations, conservation of atoms and reaction classes.
7. **Stoichiometry:** mole concept, molar mass, ratios from balanced equations, limiting reagent and yield at staged levels.
8. **Solutions and concentration:** solute/solvent, concentration, dilution and solution calculations.
9. **Acids, bases and equilibrium foundations:** pH meaning, neutralisation, reversible processes and equilibrium concepts, with mathematical depth staged carefully.
10. **Energy and rates:** exothermic/endothermic change, energy profiles, collision model and factors affecting reaction rate.
11. **Experimental reasoning:** variables, controls, measurement quality, data tables, graphing, uncertainty, reproducibility and lab report structure.
12. **Chemistry in context:** materials, food chemistry, environment, batteries and everyday chemical claims evaluated using evidence.

## Explicit boundaries

- **Mathematics domain:** owns general arithmetic, algebra, functions, proportional reasoning, graph interpretation and statistics. Chemistry owns applying those tools to chemical quantities and models.
- **Electronics domain:** owns circuit behaviour, components and electrical measurement. Electrochemistry and battery chemistry belong here only where chemical mechanisms are the learning target; circuit models remain ELEC.
- **Body Science:** owns human biochemistry and physiology in biological context. General molecular structure, reactions and quantitative chemistry remain in chemistry.
- **Practical Independence:** owns safe household handling and product-label literacy. Chemical principles and laboratory methods remain in chemistry.

## Prerequisite candidates

Number sense, fractions/ratios, unit conversion, introductory algebra, scientific measurement, graph reading and basic particle model. These are recommended foundations, not blanket hard gates; diagnostic placement should identify missing prerequisites.

## Entry pathway proposal

1. Matter, properties and measurement.
2. Atoms, ions and periodic organisation.
3. Formulae and chemical language.
4. Bonding and structure.
5. Conservation and balancing equations.
6. Mole and stoichiometric ratios.
7. Solutions and concentration.
8. Energy, reaction rates and equilibrium foundations.
9. Experimental design, uncertainty and evidence.

## Safety and age-appropriate scope

The initial pathway is conceptual and simulation/observation-led. No home synthesis, heating, mixing household chemicals, gas generation, corrosive handling, or unsupervised wet-lab work. Practical laboratory activities require a qualified teacher, appropriate PPE, risk assessment, ventilation, approved materials and institutional procedures. Never mix cleaning products.

## Proposed integration

Link to MATH for ratios, algebra, functions and statistics; RES for evidence and reproducibility; ELEC for electrochemistry/battery interfaces; BODY for biochemistry; INDEP for safe product-label interpretation. These are proposed typed links, not yet canonical edges.

## Decision required

Approve this as a new working-domain candidate under MATH, decide whether its canonical ownership remains MATH or becomes a separate CHEM discipline in a future taxonomy revision, then allocate stable IDs through the normal ID registry. Do not create duplicate chemistry pathways under both MATH and BODY/ELEC.
