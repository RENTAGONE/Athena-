## Resource selection principle
The module objective is primary. Discover and select resources that directly teach the objective at the learner's level, then build the lesson around that content. Existing resource-register entries are optional provenance leads, not a whitelist. Paid resources may be optional enrichment but must not be a required dependency when an accessible substitute can teach the outcome. Search briefs are not proof of source selection or full source verification.

## Curriculum and mastery rules
- Recommended sequence is advisory; diagnostic evidence can justify skipping, reordering or a narrow bridge.
- No hard prerequisites were added in Wave 07.
- Mastery is a profile of outcome performance, reasoning, independence, transfer and delayed retention; no universal score is calculated.
- Exposure, recall, explanation, guided performance, independent demonstration, transfer and retention remain distinct.
