The learner workflow remains: ATHENA assigns objectives and evidence criteria; learner studies through chosen external sources; learner submits explanations and demonstrations; an independent evaluator checks correctness and limitations; a structured proposed update is produced; learner approves before engine commit. Mastery is never awarded automatically by lesson completion or AI assertion.
