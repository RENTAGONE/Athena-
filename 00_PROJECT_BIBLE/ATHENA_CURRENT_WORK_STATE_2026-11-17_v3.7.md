## Non-negotiable learning workflow
ATHENA assigns objectives and evidence contracts. The learner studies through selected external sources, records provenance, explains from memory and applies the knowledge. Evaluation checks accuracy, reasoning, transfer, source quality, uncertainty and safety. A proposed update is validated and previewed; the learner explicitly approves any persistent commit. No automatic mastery and no silent curriculum updates.
