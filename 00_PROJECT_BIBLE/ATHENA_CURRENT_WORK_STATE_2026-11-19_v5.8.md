## Resource selection principle
The module objective is primary. Discover and select resources that directly teach the objective at the learner's level, then build the lesson around that content. Existing resource-register entries are optional provenance leads, not a whitelist. Paywalled material may be optional enrichment but must not be a required dependency when an accessible substitute can teach the outcome. Search briefs are not proof of source selection or full source verification.
