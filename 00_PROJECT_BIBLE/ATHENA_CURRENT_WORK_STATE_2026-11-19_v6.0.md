## Wave 09 class rules
- `personal_use`: solve a real learner-owned problem; no sale, customer or public release required.
- `product_business_foundation`: build a useful prototype/foundation with a credible possible future path to packaging and potential sale; no revenue or sale required.
- `intrinsic_exploration`: create/investigate for its own sake; utility, audience, monetisation and transfer are not required.
- These are exactly three distinct project classes. Teamwork and presentation are formats, not additional classes.
- Project completion never automatically awards module competence, transfer, retention or mastery.
