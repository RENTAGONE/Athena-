## Engineering & Computer Science (`ATH-DISC-ENG`)
**Primary purpose:** Build and maintain computational systems: software, algorithms, applications and computer systems.
**Includes:** Programming languages, software architecture, algorithms, APIs, databases, testing, operating-system concepts and computing theory.
**Excludes or defers:** Electrical circuit design, general digital safety, business strategy or visual-art technique except where directly required by a software product.
**Common overlaps:** ATH-DISC-WORK, ATH-DISC-CYBER, ATH-DISC-ELEC, ATH-DISC-AEST, ATH-DISC-BIZ
**Ownership rule:** This discipline owns the learning objective when its primary outcome is the capability described in its purpose boundary. Shared application is represented with typed links, not duplicated canonical objects.

## Global boundary rules
- Choose one canonical owner for each concept or skill based on its primary learning outcome.
- Create aliases or context-specific applications instead of duplicating the same concept in multiple disciplines.
- Use SUPPORTS, TRANSFER_TO, APPLIES_TO or RELATED_TO edges to represent overlap.
- When two disciplines genuinely teach different aspects of a shared topic, create distinct objectives and state the boundary explicitly.
- Safety-critical content may require specialist sources, supervision or certification; ATHENA content does not confer professional qualification.
- Boundary decisions are revisable through a documented change, not silent taxonomy moves.
